Before UFC 329, fans on Good Fights hyped Conor McGregor vs Max Holloway 2 at an average of 9.5 out of 10. It was the most anticipated fight of the year by our numbers, ahead of two title unifications and a White House card.

Then McGregor's knee gave out on a kick attempt 69 seconds in, Holloway landed a right hand, and the referee waved it off.

Fans came back and rated the fight 1.3 out of 10. Of the 52 people who scored it, 46 gave it a 1. That 8.2-point collapse between expectation and reality is the largest we have ever measured, and it got us wondering: which other fights promised the most and delivered the least this year?

So we ran the numbers on every fight of 2026.

## How we measured it

Good Fights users score every fight twice: a hype rating (1 to 10) before the fight, and a quality rating (1 to 10) after they watch it. That gives every fight two honest numbers, one for the promise and one for the delivery, from the same community of fans.

For this piece we took every completed fight from January 1 to July 17, 2026, kept the ones with at least 5 hype votes and 5 post-fight ratings, and computed the gap. 74 fights qualified. Sample sizes are listed with every fight below.

One finding before the list: across all 74 qualifying fights, the average hype score was 7.5 and the average post-fight rating was 6.6. Call it the hype tax. The typical 2026 fight delivered about one point less than fans expected.

**1. [Conor McGregor vs Max Holloway 2](/fights/conor-mcgregor-vs-max-holloway-2)** · UFC 329, July 11
Hyped **9.5**/10 (39 votes) · Rated **1.3**/10 (52 ratings) · Gap **-8.2**
The comeback five years in the making lasted 69 seconds. McGregor slipped on a running kick, tried another head kick, and his right knee buckled under him. Holloway finished the sequence and the night was over before it started. 46 of 52 fans rated it a 1.

**2. [Dominick Reyes vs Johnny Walker](/fights/dominick-reyes-vs-johnny-walker)** · UFC 327, April 11
Hyped **8.9**/10 (8 votes) · Rated **1.9**/10 (37 ratings) · Gap **-7.0**
On paper, two of the most violent finishers at light heavyweight. In the cage, three rounds of circling and range-finding that had the Miami crowd chanting "this is boring" before the split decision was read.

**3. [Michael Page vs Sam Patterson](/fights/michael-page-vs-sam-patterson)** · UFC London, March 21
Hyped **8.5**/10 (6 votes) · Rated **1.6**/10 (14 ratings) · Gap **-6.9**
MVP is one of the sport's great highlight machines, which is exactly why a 39-total-significant-strikes decision win, with zero takedowns from either man, landed this hard. The O2 booed it. His own peers criticized it afterward.

**4. [Max Holloway vs Charles Oliveira 2](/fights/max-holloway-vs-charles-oliveira-2)** · UFC 326, March 7
Hyped **9.3**/10 (11 votes) · Rated **4.6**/10 (31 ratings) · Gap **-4.7**
The third most hyped fight of the year. Oliveira took Holloway down in all five rounds and won 50-45 across the board. A grappling masterclass, and fans respected it more than they enjoyed it.

**5. [Rob Font vs Raul Rosas Jr.](/fights/rob-font-vs-raul-rosas-jr)** · UFC 326, March 7
Hyped **6.9**/10 (8 votes) · Rated **2.4**/10 (14 ratings) · Gap **-4.4**
Five takedowns in round one tells you most of what you need to know. Rosas swept the cards 30-27 in a dominant, one-sided wrestling display.

**6. [Ronda Rousey vs Gina Carano](/fights/ronda-rousey-vs-gina-carano)** · MVP MMA, May 16
Hyped **8.3**/10 (11 votes) · Rated **4.4**/10 (16 ratings) · Gap **-3.9**
Seventeen seconds. Takedown, mount, armbar, tap. It was the most predictable outcome in MMA, it went exactly the way everyone said it would, and fans still felt short-changed by a main event that ended before their couch cushions warmed up.

**7. [Cody Garbrandt vs Xiao Long](/fights/cody-garbrandt-vs-xiao-long)** · UFC 326, March 7
Hyped **7.6**/10 (7 votes) · Rated **4.3**/10 (15 ratings) · Gap **-3.3**
A chaotic prelim decided partly by a two-point deduction for repeated low blows. Mostly remembered for Garbrandt vomiting into a bucket in the cage afterward, which is rarely the sign of a classic.

**8. [Movsar Evloev vs Lerone Murphy](/fights/movsar-evloev-vs-lerone-murphy)** · UFC London, March 21
Hyped **8.0**/10 (5 votes) · Rated **4.9**/10 (19 ratings) · Gap **-3.1**
A 25-minute unbeaten-vs-unbeaten fight that came down to a low-blow point deduction and late takedowns. Evloev's 20th straight win, and a majority decision that satisfied almost nobody.

**9. [Malcolm Wellmaker vs Juan Diaz](/fights/malcolm-wellmaker-vs-juan-diaz)** · UFC Kansas City, May 16
Hyped **8.2**/10 (5 votes) · Rated **5.2**/10 (9 ratings) · Gap **-3.0**
The hype was for Wellmaker, one of the year's buzziest prospects. Diaz dropped him late in round one and choked him out in round two. An upset can be a great fight for the neutral, but hype money was on the other corner.

**10. [Sean Brady vs Joaquin Buckley 2](/fights/sean-brady-vs-joaquin-buckley-2)** · UFC 328, May 9
Hyped **7.6**/10 (17 votes) · Rated **4.7**/10 (30 ratings) · Gap **-2.9**
Brady took Buckley down in every round and won 30-25 on two cards. Completely effective, thoroughly one-sided, and rated accordingly.

## The letdown card of the year: UFC 326

One event shows up four times in the list above. UFC 326: Holloway vs Oliveira carried a 9.3-hype main event and produced the fourth, fifth, and seventh biggest letdowns of the year, plus the main event itself at number four. If you felt flat leaving that card, the data says you were not alone.

## The fights that saved the year

The gap runs both ways, and the overdeliveries are their own story.

**[Alexa Grasso vs Maycee Barber](/fights/alexa-grasso-vs-maycee-barber)** (UFC Seattle, March 28) was the most slept-on fight of 2026: hyped 5.3, rated 8.6 after Grasso starched Barber with a left hand and added a choke for insurance inside one round.

**[Zhang Mingyang vs Alonzo Menifield](/fights/zhang-mingyang-vs-alonzo-menifield)** (UFC Vegas, May 30) went from a 6.0 hype score to an 8.5 rating when Menifield dropped the rising star twice in four minutes.

And then there is the unicorn. **[Ilia Topuria vs Justin Gaethje](/fights/ilia-topuria-vs-justin-gaethje)** at UFC Freedom 250 came in with a 9.3 hype score, the same expectation level as McGregor vs Holloway, and fans rated it 9.7. It is the highest-rated fight of 2026 on Good Fights, a four-round war in front of the White House that ended with Topuria unable to answer the fifth-round bell. Out of the ten most hyped fights of the year, it is the only one that outdelivered its hype.

That is the quiet takeaway in this data. Hype is usually a debt the fight has to pay back, and most can't. The ones that can are why we keep watching.
