The UFC brings a numbered card back to Philadelphia for the first time in over a decade, and it does it with two championship fights on top. **UFC 330 goes down on Saturday, August 15, 2026** at the Xfinity Mobile Arena, headlined by pound-for-pound king Islam Makhachev defending his welterweight title against Ian Machado Garry, with Mackenzie Dern putting her strawweight belt on the line against Gillian Robertson in the co-main. Here is everything you need: the full card, the latest odds, start times, how to watch, and our honest read on the main event. *You can rate how hyped you are for every fight on this card on [Good Fights](https://goodfights.app).*

## When is UFC 330?

UFC 330 takes place on **Saturday, August 15, 2026**, with the main card starting at **9 p.m. ET / 6 p.m. PT**. It is the UFC's first numbered event in Philadelphia since UFC 133 back in 2011, and its first trip to the city of any kind since 2019, so the local demand is real. The main card headlines a big night of two title fights.

## Where is UFC 330 being held?

UFC 330 is at the **Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania**, the downtown arena in the South Philadelphia sports complex. Expect a loud, invested crowd: Philadelphia is one of the best fight towns in the country, and a rare numbered card in the city will bring the building to life.

## What time does UFC 330 start?

The **main card starts at 9 p.m. ET / 6 p.m. PT**, with the Makhachev walkout as the final fight of the night. UFC 330 follows the standard numbered-card structure across three broadcast segments, so expect the prelims in the early evening and the early prelims in the late afternoon Eastern. The exact prelim times firm up closer to the event, so check the confirmed schedule the week of the fight before you plan around it.

## How do I watch UFC 330?

In the United States, UFC 330 streams on **Paramount+**. As with the rest of the UFC's numbered cards now, the event is included with a subscription rather than sold as a separate hundred-dollar pay-per-view, so a single Paramount+ plan gets you the full night from the early prelims to both title fights. See the by-country section below for international options.

## Is UFC 330 a pay-per-view? How much does it cost?

In the US, no. UFC numbered events like UFC 330 are now bundled into a **Paramount+ subscription** (around $8.99 per month for the Essential tier), with no extra pay-per-view fee on top. That is a major change from the old model and makes a two-title-fight card one of the best values in combat sports right now. Several countries, including Canada and Australia, still sell UFC numbered cards on a pay-per-view basis, which we cover below.

## Who is Islam Makhachev?

Makhachev is, by most reckonings, the best fighter in the world. The former longtime lightweight champion moved up and captured the welterweight title, adding a second division to a resume that already included a long, dominant title reign and a 28-1 record. His game is suffocating: elite grappling, underrated striking, and a control-heavy style that drowns opponents. He is the standard every 155 and 170-pound contender is measured against.

## Who is Ian Machado Garry?

Garry is one of the most talked-about rising stars in the sport, a supremely confident Irish welterweight who has built a 17-1 record and a real following on the back of a sharp, rangy striking game and a knack for the spotlight. This is the biggest fight of his life and the moment his talent gets measured against the very top of the division. Love him or not, he backs himself completely, and a title shot against the pound-for-pound king is exactly the stage he has been chasing.

## Who wins Makhachev vs Garry? Our prediction

The oddsmakers see a mismatch, and the logic is hard to argue. Makhachev's grappling is the single most dominant tool in the sport, and Garry has yet to face anyone who can take him down and hold him there the way the champion can. Garry's best path is to keep it standing, use his length and volume, and make Makhachev work through range for as long as possible.

**Prediction: Makhachev by decision or late submission, unless Garry can keep it standing for twenty-five minutes, which is a very tall order against this champion.** It is a huge opportunity for Garry, but he has to solve the hardest puzzle in the division to take it. For the co-main, the champion Dern is favored, but Robertson is a live, finish-hunting underdog who makes it interesting on the mat.

## Is Mackenzie Dern vs Gillian Robertson a title fight?

Yes. The co-main event is a **women's strawweight title fight**, with champion Mackenzie Dern defending against Gillian Robertson. It is a compelling stylistic clash of two of the best grapplers in the division: Dern, a decorated jiu-jitsu black belt with heavy hands developing around her ground game, against Robertson, one of the most active submission finishers on the roster. Whoever ends up on top, this one has real finish potential.

## Why are there two title fights at UFC 330?

Because the UFC wanted to make its Philadelphia return an event, and two belts on one card does exactly that. Stacking a pound-for-pound headliner with a second championship in the co-main gives UFC 330 the kind of top-heavy billing usually reserved for the biggest weekends of the year, and it gives fans two genuine stakes-on-the-line fights instead of one.

## What is the best fight on the UFC 330 undercard?

There is real quality below the titles. **Erin Blanchfield vs Jasmine Jasudavicius** is a meaningful women's flyweight fight with rankings implications, **Edson Barboza vs Esteban Ribovics** pairs a beloved veteran finisher with a rising action fighter in a lightweight bout that should produce fireworks, and **Vicente Luque** is always in a war. Barboza vs Ribovics is our early pick for the undercard fight most likely to steal the show.
