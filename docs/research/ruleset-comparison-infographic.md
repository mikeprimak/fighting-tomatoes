## COMPLETED DATA (condensed; full citations below each)

### UFC — MMA, Unified Rules
- **Surface:** Octagon cage, 30 ft (25 ft at APEX), fence ~5'9".
- **Rounds:** 3×5 (non-title), 5×5 (title + main events). 1-min rest.
- **Gloves:** ~4 oz open-finger.
- **Strikes:** Elbows legal incl. **12-to-6 (legalized, effective Nov 1 2024)**. NO knees/kicks/stomps to grounded opponent's head. NO soccer kicks. Headbutts illegal.
- **Grounded def (changed Nov 2024):** any body part other than hands/feet on canvas (hand down no longer = grounded).
- **Ground:** full grappling/submissions; only small-joint manipulation banned.
- **Scoring:** 10-point must, 3 judges. Effective striking/grappling → aggression → control.
- **Weight:** 9 classes 115–265 lb; day-before weigh-in (~24h); **NO hydration test**.
- **Distinctive:** no grounded head strikes; no hydration/walking-weight system; trademarked Octagon.
- ⚠️ 12-6 legality is commission-by-commission; UFC.com page still lists it as a foul. Phrase as "legalized under 2024 Unified Rules, adoption varies."
- Sources: ufc.com/unified-rules, ufc.com/octagon, ABC Unified Rules July-2024 PDF, NSAC NRUC 2024, CBS/ESPN on 2024 changes.

### ONE Championship — MMA, own Global Ruleset
- **Surface:** has used both cage and ring; **no official published dimensions — do NOT print a size.**
- **Rounds:** 3×5 / 5×5 title. 4 oz gloves.
- **Strikes:** **Knees to grounded opponent's head = LEGAL** (headline distinctive). Body/leg kicks to grounded legal. **Soccer kicks BANNED (since 2016) — common myth that they're legal.** Head stomps illegal. All elbows incl 12-6 legal.
- **Grounded def:** any weight-bearing part other than soles of feet (hand down = grounded).
- **Scoring:** **WHOLE-FIGHT, not round-by-round; no 10-point-must.** Criteria: near-finish → damage → combinations/control → takedowns → aggression. 3 judges. Designed to avoid draws.
- **Weight:** **Walking-weight system + hydration test (urine SG ≤1.025), tested across fight week; dehydration cuts & IVs banned.** Born from 2015 death of Yang Jian Bing.
- **Distinctive:** grounded knees legal; whole-fight scoring; walking-weight/hydration system.
- Sources: onefc.com/martial-arts, Colorado-filed ONE ruleset (combatsportslaw.com), groundedmma.com, fightful (soccer-kick ban), bleacherreport (hydration origin).

### RIZIN — MMA, PRIDE-derived (Japan)
- **Surface:** **square roped RING, not a cage** (size unknown — don't print).
- **Rounds:** **10 min + 5 min (2 rounds) OR 3×5** per bout; women 3×5. **10-min first round still current** (Wikipedia's "abolished 2018" is WRONG per live official page). Rest period unknown.
- **Gloves:** no reliable oz figure — don't print.
- **Strikes:** **Soccer kicks, knees to grounded head, stomps all LEGAL.** **Elbows incl 12-6 LEGAL (unlike PRIDE, which banned head elbows — do NOT assume "no elbows").** Headbutts/back-of-head illegal. 15 kg+ weight gap → lighter fighter may opt out of grounded head strikes.
- **Scoring:** whole-fight (PRIDE-style); Damage/Aggression/Generalship ≈ 50/30/20 (weights from secondary sources). Draws possible.
- **Weight:** metric (kg) classes 47–120 kg + openweight. Day-before weigh-in.
- **Distinctive:** ring not cage; 10-min first round; PRIDE ground strikes BUT elbows allowed.
- ⚠️ Only authoritative source is Japanese (jp.rizinff.com/rule). Unknown: glove oz, ring size, rest period, rope-escape rule, hydration.

### MVP MMA — MMA, Unified Rules / CSAC
- Inaugural event MVP MMA 01: Rousey vs Carano, May 16 2026, Intuit Dome (Inglewood CA), Netflix.
- **Standard Unified Rules MMA, CSAC-sanctioned. NO distinctive in-cage rules.**
- **Surface:** six-sided **hexagon** cage (branding only; Unified-legal).
- **Rounds:** 3×5 undercard, 5×5 main/co-main. 4 oz gloves. 10-point must.
- **12-6 elbows legal (2026, post-Nov-2024 change) — don't list as foul.**
- **Distinctiveness is purely promotional:** Netflix (peaked ~17M viewers), celebrity/legacy matchups, fighter pay (~$40K min), sponsorship freedom.
- ⚠️ Main event = 145 lb featherweight (not "catchweight"). Inglewood not "LA."
- Sources: MVP press release, NESN, ESPN, Yahoo, CSAC suspensions/payouts/appeal (regulatory tells confirm sanctioning).

### Karate Combat — full-contact karate
- **Surface:** **"The Pit"** — sunken 6.5m × 6.5m square, 45° padded sloped walls, no cage/ropes. **Walls are in-play/scoreable, NOT "the ground."**
- **Rounds:** 3×3 (non-title), 5×3 (title/main). 1-min rest. **4 oz mitts.**
- **Strikes:** punches, kicks, ridge-hands, **elbows legal**. No kicking knees, no spine/back-of-head, no kicking a grounded opponent's head.
- **Ground (key distinctive):** **stand-up by design.** Most takedowns illegal (exceptions: catching a kick, genuine scramble). **~5-second ground cap — ref counts 5→1 then stands them up.** Top fighter may place only ONE knee down. Submissions exist (tap) but not the offense set.
- **Scoring:** 10-point must, 3 judges, **rewards "productive aggressiveness"**; Pit control (wall play) scored. Draw/split → one "Sudden Victory" round.
- **Weight:** 10 classes (W 115–150, M 135–205+).
- **Distinctive:** The Pit + sloped walls; stand-up by design (5-sec cap); karate striking w/ aggression-weighted scoring.
- Note: 2020 "virtual arena" era was production only, not a rules change.
- Sources: karate.com/en/rules-and-regulations, 2021 official PDF (ISKA mirror), Wikipedia, lockerroom.in.

### Dirty Boxing Championship (DBX) — boxing + MMA strikes, stand-up only
- Co-founded by **Mike Perry & Jon Jones**; launched 2024, DBX 1 March 2025; Miami; free on YouTube.
- **Surface:** **ring, small 18'×18' "DRING™"** (smaller to force action).
- **Rounds:** 3×3 (non-title), 5×3 (title). **5 oz MMA gloves** (rulebook; marketing says 4 oz).
- **Strikes:** punches + **elbows, ridge-hands, backfists, spinning backfists, Superman punches, palm/forearm**. **NO kicks** (removed deliberately). **NO knees** (inferred). Headbutts illegal.
- **CLINCH (corrects common assumption):** clinching/holding is **PENALIZED** — full **point deducted for "hugging or stalling"**; ref breaks inactive clinches. Tie up only to immediately strike, then break. NOT "free clinch fighting."
- **Ground:** **stand-up only, no grappling/takedowns/submissions.** Limited **standing ground-and-pound** allowed on a downed opponent while striker stays upright.
- **Scoring:** 10-point must, 3 judges, aggression-weighted. Tie → extra round (split decisions "abolished" per marketing; rulebook still lists Split).
- **Weight:** 9 classes 125–205+.
- **Distinctive:** MMA strikes in a boxing ring, no kicks/no grappling; anti-clinch/anti-stall point deduction; no split decisions (extra round).
- ⚠️ Get the clinch right — it's the most likely Reddit "gotcha." Glove = 5 oz (rulebook) vs 4 oz (marketing).
- Sources: dirtyboxingchampionship.com/rules + /about, DBX 2 PRNewswire, Newsweek (Perry interview), Yardbarker (point-deduction change).

### PFL — MMA, Unified Rules + season/points format
- **Surface:** **SmartCage** — standard octagonal cage, branded (sensor/stat tech); Unified-legal dimensions.
- **Rounds:** 3×5 (regular season + playoffs), 5×5 championship final. ~4 oz gloves. 10-point must **per fight** (in-cage scoring is standard Unified).
- **Strikes:** standard US Unified — same as UFC (incl. 12-6 legalized 2024). NO grounded head strikes, NO soccer kicks. No distinctive in-cage rules.
- **Distinctive = the SEASON FORMAT, not the fighting.** Regular season: 6 weight classes (HW, LHW, WW, LW, FW, W-FLW), ~10 fighters each, **each fighter fights twice**. Points: **win = 3, draw = 1, loss = 0**, PLUS finish bonus by round (R1 stoppage = +3, R2 = +2, R3 = +1) — often summarized as **6/5/4 pts for a R1/R2/R3 finish, 3 for a decision win**. Top 4 per division → playoffs → final. Season champ wins **$1 million**.
- ⚠️ PFL **revamped the format for the 2025 season** (more bracket-style playoffs, away from the pure two-bout regular season). The 3-points-+-finish-bonus system above is the classic 2018–2024 model — **verify the exact current-season scoring in Phase 2** before printing specifics; the *concept* (regular season points → playoffs → $1M) is stable.
- Sources: verdictmma.com/pfl-format-explained, thesportster.com PFL scoring, AOL/ESPN on 2025 format change.

### Oktagon — European MMA, "CSMMA" Unified Rules
- **Ruleset:** **CSMMA professional rules** = the Unified Rules of MMA framework, **approved by Oktagon's referee commission effective Jan 1 2025.** Conservative/European reading of Unified.
- **Surface:** standard cage. **Rounds:** 3×5 / 5×5 title, ~4 oz gloves, 10-point must (all standard Unified).
- **Strikes:** Unified-standard, with **two notable conservative differences vs current UFC/US Unified** (the real "gotchas"):
  1. **12-to-6 (straight downward) elbow REMAINS ILLEGAL.** (US Unified legalized it Nov 2024; Oktagon still bans it — only *angled* elbows are legal.)
  2. **Grounded definition is the OLDER one — a single hand on the canvas does NOT make you grounded** (need both hands palm/fist down, or another body part touching). This is the opposite of UFC's Nov-2024 change (where a hand down = grounded).
  - Knees/kicks to a grounded opponent's head: **illegal** (standard). Stomping a grounded fighter: illegal (axe kicks and standing foot-stomps are NOT stomps). No soccer kicks.
- **Distinctive:** the rules are *conservative Unified* (12-6 still banned, hand-down ≠ grounded). Org identity is **tournament/bracket-driven** — e.g. **Tipsport Gamechanger** (16 European fighters, **€1M**, 5-event pyramid). Regional: Czechia/Slovakia/Germany/Spain.
- Sources: oktagonmma.com/en/csmma-professional-rules (scraped 2026-06; CS/DE/EN trilingual rulebook).

### BKFC — bare-knuckle boxing
- **Surface:** the **"Squared Circle"** — a *circular* ring marked with **scratch lines** (Broughton Rules heritage, 19th c.).
- **Rounds:** **5 × 2 min** (title fights **7 × 2 min**). **No gloves;** wraps/tape **not allowed within 1 inch of the knuckle** (true bare knuckle — its literal differentiator).
- **Strikes:** **PUNCHES ONLY** — straights, hooks, uppercuts, overhands. **NO elbows, knees, kicks, headbutts, grappling, takedowns, or submissions.**
- **Toe-the-line:** every round **starts with both fighters toeing the scratch lines, inches apart, face to face** (forced engagement).
- **Scoring:** 10-point must, 3 judges.
- **Weight:** **9 classes**, Strawweight **107 lb** → Super Heavyweight **266+ lb**.
- **Distinctive:** genuinely bare (no wrap near the knuckle); toe-the-line round starts; punches only in a circular ring.
- Sources: bkfc.com/about, boxbets.co.uk BKFC rules, unsanctionedfights.com BKFC 2026 guide.

### RAF — Real American Freestyle (wrestling, no striking)
- **What it is:** pro **freestyle wrestling** league, **founded 2025** (Chad Bronstein, coach Israel "Izzy" Martinez, Hulk Hogan). **Hogan = commissioner, Eric Bischoff = Chief Media Officer.** The **only striking-free org** on the list.
- **Surface:** **wrestling mat** (circle) — no cage, no ropes.
- **Periods:** **3 × 2 min**, 30-sec breaks between periods.
- **Scoring:** freestyle points (takedowns / exposure / control). **Fall (PIN) = both shoulders held to the mat → instant win, match over.** **Technical Fall = 10-point lead → match stopped.** Highest score at end wins; **ties broken by criteria (last point scored wins).**
- **No striking, no submissions** (freestyle wrestling, not catch/MMA).
- **Distinctive:** pure pro freestyle wrestling; pin or 10-point tech fall ends it; the outlier discipline (frame the infographic around it being "not a fight" in the striking sense).
- Sources: realamericanfreestyle.com rules explainer, en.wikipedia.org RAF champions/RAF 07–08, nxtbets.com RAF betting guide.

## Cross-cutting "gotchas" found (use these as the infographic's surprises)
- 12-to-6 elbows are now LEGAL in Unified-Rules MMA (Nov 2024) — UFC/MVP.
- ONE does NOT allow soccer kicks (myth) — its real edge is grounded knees + whole-fight scoring + hydration system.
- RIZIN allows elbows incl 12-6 (unlike its PRIDE ancestor) and keeps the 10-min first round.
- DBX penalizes the clinch (opposite of "dirty boxing" intuition).
- **Oktagon (CSMMA) is the conservative mirror of UFC:** it still BANS the 12-6 elbow that the US legalized in 2024, AND a single hand on the canvas does NOT make you grounded (UFC reversed this in Nov 2024). Same base ruleset, two opposite calls.
- **PFL's "rules" aren't in the cage — they're the standings.** Standard Unified fighting; the distinctive is a points-based regular season → playoffs → $1M, like no other MMA org.
- **BKFC is the only org where wrapping near the knuckle is illegal** (others that say "bare knuckle" still wrap) + toe-the-line forced round starts.
- **RAF is the odd one out entirely** — freestyle wrestling, no striking, pin = instant win. Good "one of these is not like the others" infographic hook.
- Competition surfaces span the whole spread: Octagon (UFC) / SmartCage (PFL) / hexagon cage (MVP) / standard cage (Oktagon) / circular roped ring (ONE has used both) / square roped ring (RIZIN) / small "DRING" ring (DBX) / circular "Squared Circle" (BKFC) / The Pit, sloped walls (Karate Combat) / wrestling mat (RAF).
