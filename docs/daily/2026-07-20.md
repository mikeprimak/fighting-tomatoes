### New reusable asset: `packages/web/public/brand/good-fights-stacked-horizontal.png`

Built by **`packages/backend/scripts/buildStackedLogo.js`** from `THICKER-GOOD-FIGHTS-LOGO-HAND-AND-WORD-TALLER.png` (repo root). That source is yellow artwork on **solid near-black with no usable alpha**, stacked hand-over-words, so dropping it on a dark canvas would show a visible rectangle. The script keys the background out by converting **luminance to alpha, un-premultiplied** (so the yellow keeps saturation and edges stay anti-aliased), splits hand from wordmark at y=1470, trims each to its content box, and recomposes them side by side. Re-runnable; `FLOOR` controls the key threshold (48 kills the source's faint noise specks).

Gotcha logged: **sharp will not chain `extract()` and `trim()` in one pipeline** — round-trip through a buffer between them or it throws `bad extract area`.
