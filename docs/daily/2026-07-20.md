## Session 2 — banner rebuild + media list

### New banner: the "tile wall" (`0a258c1b`)

Mike rejected the original hero as empty/plain/unbranded. Diagnosis: ~40% dead space, no focal point, and the big gold 9.5 bar was the visual hero — the image accidentally celebrated the hype when the story is the collapse.

Replacement concept: **one tile per individual fan rating**, coloured with the app's own heatmap (`packages/web/src/utils/heatmap.ts`: 1 = grey `rgb(128,128,128)` → 7 yellow → 8 orange → 9-10 red). A letdown renders as a wall of dead grey with a single warm outlier. **This is the Round Numbers series device from here on** — a great fight will render as a wall of fire using the identical scale.

Generator: `packages/backend/scripts/generateLetdownsBanner.js` (puppeteer → PNG, hero 1600x900 + OG 1200x630). Re-runnable; edit `SCORES` for the next installment.

**Deliberately no fighter photo.** The `conor-ufc329-arms-up.png` in the repo root is a professional arena shot (Getty/Zuffa). Using it unlicensed is exposure on its own, but the article footer explicitly grants journalists republication rights — embedding a licensed photo under that sentence would be granting rights we don't hold, in writing, to press. If a fighter image is ever wanted: licensed Getty single-image buy, illustration, or own photography.

### Clickable table of contents (`fd02253c`)

marked v18 renders bare `<h2>`/`<h3>` with **no id**, so in-page anchors had nothing to target. Rather than override marked's renderer (version-fragile), `addHeadingIds()` in `packages/web/src/lib/posts.ts` injects slug ids into the parsed HTML, with `scroll-margin-top:88px` so anchored headings clear the sticky header. Duplicate slugs get a numeric suffix; hand-written HTML headings that already carry an id are left alone.

**This applies to every post on the site**, not just this one — all blog sections are now deep-linkable, which is also a small SEO win (Google can surface jump-to-section links).

Letdowns article gets a 10-item TOC below the TLDR. All 10 anchors were verified against the generated ids before push (`BROKEN: none`), and confirmed live: 10 heading ids, 10 TOC links.
